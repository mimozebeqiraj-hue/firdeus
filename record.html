Record the words of the prayer

Jazakallahu khayran for helping. These recordings will teach new Muslims to say every word of the prayer, line by line, until they know it by heart.

Before you start
  • A quiet room. Hold the phone about 20 cm from your mouth.
  • Recite slowly and clearly, the way you would teach a beginner, with correct tajwid.
  • Say each line once, then press Stop. Leave a short moment of silence at the start and end.
  • Listen back. If you are not happy, press Record again to redo it.
  • Your recordings are kept on this phone until you send them, so you can stop and continue later.
